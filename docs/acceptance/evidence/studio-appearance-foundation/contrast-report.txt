STATUS NOTICES AND DIFF ROWS
The shared muted/success/warning/info/destructive badges and the warning banner use
the semantic status fill with readable foreground text. Chat, knowledge, and schedule
notices and diff rows retain their status tint while using --foreground for body text;
the failed-turn icon keeps --destructive for its visual status cue. The destructive
dropdown item uses --foreground when focused over its tinted background.

For --foreground over each status tint composited on --card, the minimum across
all four palettes and both themes is 14.33:1 at 5% opacity, 12.93:1 at 10%, and
11.60:1 at 15%. All 96 status/tint/palette/theme combinations pass 4.5:1.
The worst original text-on-tint pairs were 3.81:1 (destructive badge in light)
and 4.13:1 (destructive/warning text on a 10% light tint); these are corrected
without changing the frozen status hues.

BRAND FOREGROUND USES
The decorative --brand fill is not used for foreground text or icons on cards.
The prior dark Default --brand measured 2.41:1 on --card; affected content now
uses --brand-ink. Across the eight palette/theme pairs, --brand-ink reaches at
least 4.92:1 on --card for text and 4.30:1 on a 10% --brand tint for icons.
Selected chat, knowledge, and schedule item borders/rings also use solid
--brand-ink; their translucent --brand backgrounds remain decorative.
